Monthly art nights · Galentine's edition

Draw on the Furniture

(but only this one, please)

Every month I invite friends over to talk and work on one shared piece. For Galentine's, the canvas was the table we were eating at. Ten friends, a lot of paint pens, food, and wine. Everyone took a corner. Afterward I filled in the space between them until it all became one landscape.

The landscape

Afterward I worked across the whole surface, connecting everyone's pieces with vines, rivers, and pattern so they read as one world.

The only rule

Why a table?

I wanted a piece nobody could be precious about. A canvas makes people freeze up. A table you've been eating dinner on doesn't.

Every friend brought her own style: tight patterns, loose florals, mushrooms, words. My job was the connective tissue, finding a structure that let all of it belong together without sanding off what made each corner hers.

It's still the table we eat at.

PowerPoint Parties

PowerPoint Party

A night of tiny lectures

1

How it works

  • Pick a topic you love
  • Make a few slides
  • Present to a living room of friends
  • Take questions (and heckles)
2

Any topic. Truly.

The more niche, the better. The best talks are the ones only you could give.

3

Why I host

My whole career has been about studying people from a distance, through data. These nights are the opposite: people up close, making things, explaining what they love, and finding the pattern that holds a room together.

They're also how a lot of my best ideas start.
